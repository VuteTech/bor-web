On 7 and 8 November, Bor goes to [OpenFest](https://www.openfest.org/2026/) in Sofia. We'll have a stand for the whole weekend, and on Saturday I'll be giving a talk about the project.

OpenFest has been the meeting point of the Bulgarian free software community for many years, and it's where a lot of us first met the people we still work with today. Bringing Bor there feels a bit like bringing it home.

## The talk

On **Saturday at 12:15 in hall "Varna"** I'll speak (in Bulgarian) about [managing Linux workstations with Bor](https://cfp.openfest.org/openfest-2026/talk/LCCN7F/).

More and more organizations are putting Linux on their desktops, and sooner or later someone gets asked the awkward question: *how do we actually manage all these machines?* I spent years as that someone. Bor is the tool I wish I'd had. The talk is about why we built it, how it works, and where it's going. Bring your questions, and your war stories too.

## The stand

If 45 minutes in a lecture hall isn't your thing, come and find us at the Bor stand on either day. We'll have Bor running live, so you can push a policy and watch it land on a desktop in real time, or just ask us anything.

We'd especially like to talk to you if you look after Linux machines at a school, a municipality, a company, or a hackerspace. Tell us what's hard about it, what Bor is missing, and what would make it useful for you. That kind of conversation shapes the roadmap more than anything else.

## See you there

OpenFest takes place at Interpred WTC Sofia, 36 Dragan Tsankov Blvd. Check the [full programme](https://cfp.openfest.org/openfest-2026/), plan your weekend, and stop by to say hello.
